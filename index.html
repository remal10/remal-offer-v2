Guest announcements

Guest reviews

Reviews become public only after validation. Moderate for relevance, privacy and abusive content, regardless of whether the rating is positive or negative. These are app reviews, not verified-stay reviews.

Guest statistics

Counts start at activation. QR openings require the tagged QR address. Unique visitors are estimated by browser, not people. Each event is counted once per browser session and offer; staff activity and browsers requesting Do Not Track are excluded. Blocked tracking and automation can affect counts.

Change history & restoration

Restore a deleted item or an earlier version. Offers and announcements return as drafts. Older media is kept for restoration. History starts with this update.

Team access

Administrators manage all sections. Section staff can edit only their section’s offers and cover. Saving access does not send an invitation email.
